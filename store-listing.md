# Store Listing: Gita Wisdom New Tab

- **Name:** Gita Wisdom New Tab
- **Summary:** Transform your new tab with Bhagavad Gita wisdom. Displays shlokas in Sanskrit, English, and Hindi with text-to-speech reading and meanings.
- **Category:** Lifestyle
- **Language:** English
- **Privacy policy URL:** https://ravitejakamalapuram.github.io/gita-wisdom.html

**Description:**
Gita Wisdom New Tab transforms your browser's new tab page into a tranquil spiritual sanctuary. Start each browsing session with timeless wisdom from the Srimad Bhagavad Gita, featuring Sanskrit shlokas, phonetic transliterations, text-to-speech reading, and commentary in English and Hindi.

KEY FEATURES:
• A Shloka Every New Tab: A verse from a curated collection of the Gita, shown in Sanskrit Devanagari script with transliteration.
• Multilingual Meanings: Word meanings, translation and commentary in English and Hindi. Other Indian languages (Telugu, Tamil, Bengali and more) are machine-translated on demand via Google Translate.
• Listen: Have the verse read aloud using your browser's built-in text-to-speech voices (voice quality depends on the voices installed on your device).
• Serene Aesthetics: Ambient visual backgrounds, tranquil starry night sky, and distraction-free dark and light themes.
• Bookmarks & Search: Save favourite verses and search the collection by keyword or theme.
• Private: No tracking, analytics, or accounts. Preferences and bookmarks are stored only on your computer. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and verse text is sent to Google Translate only when you choose a language other than English or Hindi.

HOW TO USE:
1. Install Gita Wisdom New Tab and open a new tab in Chrome.
2. Contemplate the shloka, read its translation, and listen to it read aloud.
3. Search verses and save favourites to your personal bookmarks.
4. Enjoy peace, mindfulness, and clarity with every new tab you open.

**Single purpose description:**
Gita Wisdom New Tab replaces the default new tab page with a tranquil spiritual dashboard that shows Bhagavad Gita shlokas with translations, commentary and text-to-speech reading.
